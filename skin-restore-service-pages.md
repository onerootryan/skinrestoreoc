# PART 1 — How to build these in Square Online

## Create the page
1. **Square Dashboard** → **Online** → **Website** → **Edit Site**
2. **+ Add** → **Page** → choose a **blank/standard page** (not Item or Category)
3. Name it → **Add**

## Build the content
Square builds pages from **sections**. Click **+** → **Section** and pick:

| Copy block | Section type |
|---|---|
| H1 + intro | **Hero** or **Cover** (with photo) |
| Each `H2` block | **Text** section |
| FAQs | **Accordion**, or Text with bold questions |
| Booking prompt | **Call to action** with a button |

Set the H1 as **Heading 1** and each H2 as **Heading 2** using the formatting toolbar. **One H1 per page only.**

## Set SEO fields
Page dropdown (top left) → **gear icon** → **View page settings** → **SEO** → paste the title, description and permalink → **Save**

## Publish
Top right. **Nothing goes live until you publish.** This catches everyone.

## Then
- Add **image alt text** in each image's description field
- Add the **internal links** listed at the bottom of each page
- Wire up a **Services dropdown**: Header → Navigation → Edit links

### H1: Dermaplaning in San Clemente & Orange County

No downtime, no recovery, and the smoothest your skin has felt in years.

Dermaplaning uses a sterile surgical blade held at an angle to sweep away dead surface cells and the fine vellus hair — peach fuzz — that sits on top of them. What's left is skin that catches light instead of dulling it.

### H2: Why people keep coming back for this one

It's the treatment clients are most skeptical about and most loyal to afterward.

**Makeup sits differently.** Foundation goes on smooth instead of catching on fine hair. This is the reason a lot of people book it before events.

**Products work harder.** You've just removed the barrier of dead cells that was blocking absorption. Your serums finally reach live skin.

**Instant brightness.** Vellus hair traps light and dulls the complexion. Removing it produces a noticeable change in how skin reflects light.

**As a standalone treatment — about 30 minutes**
A full appointment. We cleanse and prep properly, dermaplane the whole face in careful sections, then finish with a treatment mask and the serums your skin needs. Because dermaplaning clears the barrier of dead cells blocking absorption, that finishing step does considerably more work than it would on untreated skin — which is the whole point of doing it as a full service rather than a quick pass.

**As an add-on — about 15 minutes**
Added to the start of another treatment. Dermaplaning first means whatever comes next goes into cleared, receptive skin. It pairs particularly well with BREEZE or DiamondGlow, and it's the most common way clients build it into a regular routine.

We work in small sections with the blade held at roughly a 45-degree angle. It feels like a soft scraping — most people compare it to a very light shaving sensation. There's no pain and nothing sharp is ever pressed into the skin.

### H2: The hair question, settled

**No, it does not grow back thicker or darker.**

This is the single most common worry and it's a myth. Vellus hair is genetically programmed to a set thickness and color. Cutting it doesn't change the follicle. What people are noticing is that the blunt cut end of regrowth can feel coarser than the naturally tapered tip for a few days — it feels different, it isn't different.

Terminal hair (the coarse hair on the chin and upper lip in some people) is a separate hair type and dermaplaning doesn't affect it either.

A peel isn't one treatment — it's a category. The difference between a peel that transforms your skin and one that damages it comes down to choosing the right acid, at the right strength, for the right skin.

### H2: What a peel does

A chemical solution is applied to the skin to dissolve the bonds holding dead cells together. Those cells release and shed, revealing newer skin underneath — and the controlled exfoliation prompts your skin to increase cell turnover and collagen production going forward.

How deep that goes determines everything: the result, the recovery time and the risk.

### H2: Our peel levels

**Mild / superficial**
Targets the outermost layer only. Brightens, smooths, evens tone. Little to no visible peeling — you may just feel slightly dry for a couple of days. Good as a monthly maintenance treatment and safe before events.
*Best for:* dullness, mild congestion, general maintenance

**Moderate**
Reaches deeper into the epidermis. Expect three to five days of visible flaking. This is where you start seeing genuine change in pigmentation and texture.
*Best for:* sun damage, melasma, post-acne marks, deeper lines

**Acne-specific**
Salicylic and mandelic acid formulations that penetrate oil and clear congestion from inside the pore.
*Best for:* active breakouts, blackheads, oily congested skin

**Mandelic acid**
Worth calling out separately. Mandelic has a larger molecule than glycolic, so it penetrates more slowly and evenly — which makes it significantly safer for deeper skin tones, where aggressive peels can trigger post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation. We stock 5% and 8% mandelic for home use alongside our in-clinic treatments.
*Best for:* sensitive skin, Fitzpatrick IV–VI, rosacea-prone skin, first-time peel clients

**BioRePeel**
A 35% TCA peel with a biphasic formula designed to renew the skin with minimal visible peeling — the strength of a TCA peel, without the week of flaking.
