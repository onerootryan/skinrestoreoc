### H1: Fire and Ice Facial in San Clemente

Two masques, fifteen minutes apart, and the reason this treatment is known as the red carpet facial.

The iS Clinical Fire and Ice Facial resurfaces the skin and then immediately calms it. You get the visible result of a clinical treatment without the recovery that usually comes attached — which is why people book it before events rather than weeks ahead of them.

### H2: How it works

**The fire.** A concentrated resurfacing masque goes on first. It warms noticeably as it works, loosening dead surface cells, clearing congestion and prompting cell turnover. The warmth builds, holds, then eases. It's intense for a few minutes and completely controlled — we're watching the whole time.

**The ice.** A cooling, deeply hydrating masque follows straight away. It draws the heat out, calms any flushing, and floods the skin with hydration while the barrier is at its most receptive.

The sequence is the point. Resurfacing alone leaves skin exposed and pink. Resurfacing followed immediately by intensive recovery gives you the brightness without the aftermath.

### H2: What it helps with

- Dullness and lack of glow
- Uneven texture and rough patches
- Congestion and enlarged pores
- Fine lines
- Sun damage and uneven tone
- Skin that needs to look its best on a specific date

### H2: What to expect

**Length:** About 45 minutes
**Sensation:** Genuine warmth, building to intense for two or three minutes, then immediate cool relief. Most people find the contrast the memorable part.
**Downtime:** None. Possible pinkness for an hour.
**Results:** Visible immediately — skin looks brighter and feels noticeably smoother. Holds for two to three weeks.

### H2: Before an event

This is one of the best treatments we offer for event prep, but timing matters. **Book two to three days before**, not the morning of. That gives any pinkness time to settle while you keep the full brightness.

If your event is tomorrow, Cool Restore Cryo or a DermaPlane is the better choice.

### H2: Who shouldn't have it

- Very reactive or rosacea-prone skin in an active flare
- Active cold sores or broken skin in the area
- Isotretinoin (Accutane) within the last six months
- Recent waxing, laser or another resurfacing treatment
- Pregnancy — tell us and we'll recommend an alternative

Tell us about any retinoid or acid you've used in the last week. It changes how your skin responds.

### H2: Common questions

**Does it actually burn?**
It gets genuinely warm and can feel intense for a few minutes. It isn't painful, and the cooling masque is immediate relief. A lot of people enjoy the contrast.

**How is it different from a chemical peel?**
A peel resurfaces and then you flake for several days. Fire and Ice resurfaces and recovers within the same appointment. Less depth, no downtime.

**Can I have it if I've never had a facial before?**
Yes, though tell us — we'll talk you through each stage as it happens so nothing is a surprise.

**How often?**
Every four to six weeks as maintenance, or as a one-off before something that matters.

**Is it safe for deeper skin tones?**
Generally yes. It uses no heat device and no light, so pigmentation risk is low. We'll assess your skin first as always.
