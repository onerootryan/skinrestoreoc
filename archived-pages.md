### H1: Fire and Ice Facial in San Clemente

Two masques, fifteen minutes apart, and the reason this treatment is known as the red carpet facial.

The iS Clinical Fire and Ice Facial resurfaces the skin and then immediately calms it. You get the visible result of a clinical treatment without the recovery that usually comes attached — which is why people book it before events rather than weeks ahead of them.

### H2: How it works

**The fire.** A concentrated resurfacing masque goes on first. It warms noticeably as it works, loosening dead surface cells, clearing congestion and prompting cell turnover. The warmth builds, holds, then eases. It's intense for a few minutes and completely controlled — we're watching the whole time.

**The ice.** A cooling, deeply hydrating masque follows straight away. It draws the heat out, calms any flushing, and floods the skin with hydration while the barrier is at its most receptive.

The sequence is the point. Resurfacing alone leaves skin exposed and pink. Resurfacing followed immediately by intensive recovery gives you the brightness without the aftermath.

### H2: What it helps with

- Dullness and lack of glow
- Uneven texture and rough patches
- Congestion and enlarged pores
- Fine lines
- Sun damage and uneven tone
- Skin that needs to look its best on a specific date

### H2: What to expect

**Length:** About 45 minutes
**Sensation:** Genuine warmth, building to intense for two or three minutes, then immediate cool relief. Most people find the contrast the memorable part.
**Downtime:** None. Possible pinkness for an hour.
**Results:** Visible immediately — skin looks brighter and feels noticeably smoother. Holds for two to three weeks.

### H2: Before an event

This is one of the best treatments we offer for event prep, but timing matters. **Book two to three days before**, not the morning of. That gives any pinkness time to settle while you keep the full brightness.

If your event is tomorrow, Cool Restore Cryo or a DermaPlane is the better choice.

### H2: Who shouldn't have it

- Very reactive or rosacea-prone skin in an active flare
- Active cold sores or broken skin in the area
- Isotretinoin (Accutane) within the last six months
- Recent waxing, laser or another resurfacing treatment
- Pregnancy — tell us and we'll recommend an alternative

Tell us about any retinoid or acid you've used in the last week. It changes how your skin responds.

### H2: Pricing

Pricing is confirmed at your consultation. Call **(949) 545-7919** and we'll talk it through.

### H2: Common questions

**Does it actually burn?**
It gets genuinely warm and can feel intense for a few minutes. It isn't painful, and the cooling masque is immediate relief. A lot of people enjoy the contrast.

**How is it different from a chemical peel?**
A peel resurfaces and then you flake for several days. Fire and Ice resurfaces and recovers within the same appointment. Less depth, no downtime.

**Can I have it if I've never had a facial before?**
Yes, though tell us — we'll talk you through each stage as it happens so nothing is a surprise.

**How often?**
Every four to six weeks as maintenance, or as a one-off before something that matters.

**Is it safe for deeper skin tones?**
Generally yes. It uses no heat device and no light, so pigmentation risk is low. We'll assess your skin first as always.

### H1: Back Facial in San Clemente

The skin on your back gets the same congestion, breakouts and dullness as your face — and none of the attention, because you can't see it or reach it.

A back facial — we call it the Backcial — is a proper facial for your back: deep cleansing, exfoliation, extractions and a treatment mask, done by someone who can actually see what's going on. About thirty minutes, and no downtime.

### H2: What it helps with

- Back breakouts and congestion
- Blackheads and clogged pores across the shoulders and upper back
- Rough, bumpy or uneven texture
- Dullness and dry patches
- Marks left behind by old breakouts
- Getting your back ready for a wedding, a backless dress or a beach trip

### H2: What happens in the appointment

**Deep cleanse.** A thorough cleanse to lift oil, sweat and product buildup from skin that rarely gets properly cleaned.

**Exfoliation.** Clears away dead skin cells so pores can breathe and products can reach the skin.

**Extractions.** Congestion on the back is often deeper and more stubborn than on the face. Clearing it properly, with the right tools and pressure, is the part you can't do yourself.

**Treatment mask.** Chosen for what your skin needs — clarifying for congestion, calming for irritation, hydrating for dryness.

**Home care.** A short routine that fits into your shower, because that's where back care actually happens.

### H2: What to expect

**Length:** About 30 minutes
**Sensation:** Relaxing for most of it. Extractions can feel briefly uncomfortable.
**Downtime:** None. Mild redness where extractions were done, usually gone within a few hours.
**Results:** Skin feels smoother straight away. Breakouts improve over a series, alongside home care.

### H2: When it's more than a facial

Stubborn back breakouts sometimes have causes a facial alone can't fix — workout clothes left on too long, conditioner rinsing down your back in the shower, or hormones. We'll talk through what might be feeding it.

If your breakouts are deep, painful and cystic, a dermatologist is the right first step, and we'll tell you so.

### H2: Who shouldn't have it

- An active infection, open wounds or a sunburn on the back
- A rash or skin condition in the area that hasn't been diagnosed
- Isotretinoin (Accutane) within the last six months

### H2: Pricing

Pricing is confirmed at your consultation, once we've looked at your skin and agreed on a plan. Treatment length, the products we choose, and any add-ons all affect the cost — so we'd rather quote you accurately than guess.

Call **(949) 545-7919** and we'll talk it through.

### H2: Common questions

**How often should I get a back facial?**
Every four weeks while you're clearing breakouts, then as maintenance — or once before a particular event.

**Can I book it before a wedding or event?**
Yes. Book it about a week ahead, so any redness from extractions has well and truly settled.

**Is it only for acne?**
No. It's just as useful for rough texture, dryness, or skin that simply never gets looked after.

**Do you treat the chest too?**
Yes — chest treatments are available separately. Mention it when you book.

**Do men get back facials?**
Often. Back breakouts are common for anyone who trains, surfs or sweats regularly.

### H1: Brow Tint in San Clemente

Fuller-looking, more defined brows — without pencil, powder or anything permanent.

A brow tint applies a semi-permanent dye to your brow hairs. Fine, pale or grey hairs that disappear against your skin suddenly show up, and the shape you already have starts to look deliberate. It takes about fifteen minutes, with no recovery at all.

### H2: What a tint does

- Darkens fine, light or grey hairs so they can actually be seen
- Makes sparse brows look fuller, because hairs you couldn't see now count
- Defines your natural shape rather than drawing on a new one
- Cuts down the time you spend filling your brows in every morning

### H2: Choosing the shade

We match the tint to your hair color and skin tone. The goal is brows that look like yours on a good day — not brows that arrive before you do.

If you're unsure, we start a shade lighter. A tint can always go deeper next time, but it can't go lighter.

### H2: What to expect

**Length:** About 10–15 minutes
**Sensation:** Nothing to speak of. A little cool as the tint goes on.
**Downtime:** None. You can wear makeup straight away.
**How long it lasts:** Around three to six weeks, depending on your skin and how quickly your brow hairs cycle.

It's quick enough to add to the start of a facial, and pairs well with DermaPlane or DiamondGlow.

### H2: The patch test

Tint is a dye, and a small number of people react to dyes. If you've never had your brows tinted, or you've ever reacted to hair color, we'll do a patch test 24 to 48 hours before your appointment. It takes a minute, and it's the difference between a good result and a bad week.

### H2: Who shouldn't have it

- A known allergy or past reaction to hair dye
- Irritation, a rash or broken skin around the brows
- An eye infection, or recent eye surgery
- Anyone new to tinting who hasn't had a patch test first

### H2: Pricing

Pricing is confirmed at your appointment. Call **(949) 545-7919** and we'll talk it through.

### H2: Common questions

**How long does a brow tint last?**
Usually three to six weeks. The tint fades from the skin within a few days, then from the hairs as they naturally shed and regrow.

**Will it look too dark?**
Not if the shade is chosen properly. We match it to your coloring and start conservatively. A tint looks darkest for the first day or two, then settles.

**Can I tint my brows if I've had microblading?**
Yes — just tell us. The tint colors your natural hairs, not the pigment in your skin, and we'll match the two.

**How often should I come in?**
Every four to six weeks keeps your brows consistent.

**Can I add it to a facial?**
Yes. It's quick, and it's one of the most common add-ons. Mention it when you book.
